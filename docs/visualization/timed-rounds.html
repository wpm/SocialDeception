Timed rounds

One night and one day of Werewolf under timed pointing sessions. At night the pack, the seer and the doctor point in separate sessions that close on their own clocks. By day everyone talks and points in public until a majority casts the hammer. See ADR-0011 for the rules and ADR-0013 for speech and typing.
